ひらめき忍者と 四つの里 (Hirameki Ninja and the Four Villages)

Support

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

“ひらめき忍者と 四つの里” (Hirameki Ninja and the Four Villages) is a game for children in which you travel around four villages with the ninjas and think while looking at pictures.

What the app contains

In the four villages of Iga, Koga, Saika and Fuma, children take on training of 16 questions per village, 64 questions in total. A break is inserted every 3 questions. Each time a village is completed a lantern is lit, and when all four lights are gathered, the Hirameki Festival begins.

How to Play

  1. Look at the picture and the question, and choose the card you think is the answer.
  2. Press “この こたえに する” (Go with this answer) to check your answer.
  3. Even if you get it wrong, you can look at the clues and choose again as many times as you like.
  4. After a correct answer, you can confirm the deciding clue in the picture.

When playing for the first time, “あそびかた” (How to play), which shows the controls with pictures, is displayed. You can review it at any time from “あそびかたを 見る” (See how to play) on the title screen.

FAQ

Do I need to register an account?

No. There is no account creation or login feature.

Where is game data stored?

The ninja name, progress, parental settings and sound settings are stored only on the device. They are not sent to any external server.

Are there ads or purchases?

Neither. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no additional charges.

How do I change the parental settings?

Press “おうちのひと せってい” (Settings for grown-ups) on the title screen, then press and hold the button that appears for 3 seconds. You can change the daily limit on the number of scrolls and the hours during which the game can be played.

How do I turn off the sound?

Open “おうちのひと せってい” (Settings for grown-ups) on the title screen and press “すべての音” (All sounds) under “サウンド” (Sound) to toggle sound on and off. BGM and sound effects can be toggled individually with the buttons below it.

How do I change or delete the ninja name?

You can change it from “なまえを かえる” (Change name) on the title screen. If you save with the input field empty, you can play without a ninja name.

How do I restart progress from the beginning?

Choose “最初から やりなおす” (Start over) on the title screen and follow the instructions on the confirmation screen. Deleted progress cannot be restored.
