ひらめき忍者と 四つの里 (Hirameki Ninja and the Four Villages)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

“ひらめき忍者と 四つの里” (Hirameki Ninja and the Four Villages; the “App”) is a game in which children think while looking at pictures. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

Key points
  • There is no account registration or login.
  • No personal information is obtained within the App.
  • Play records and settings are stored only on the device and are not sent outside it.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking and no external analytics tools.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not obtain personal information such as name, address, telephone number or email address within the App. There is no account registration or login feature either.

A nickname can be used as the ninja name; the App does not ask for a real name.

2. Information Stored on the Device

So that the game can be continued from where it was left off, the App stores the following information only on the user’s device. This information is never sent to the developer’s or any third party’s server.

  • Ninja name (nickname)
  • Progress (questions solved, completion status of each village, number of scrolls for the day)
  • Parental settings (daily limit on the number of scrolls, hours during which the game can be played)
  • Sound settings (BGM, sound effects)

The ninja name can be changed or deleted at any time from the App’s title screen.

3. Provision to Third Parties

Except where required by law, the App does not provide or sell the information it handles to third parties.

4. Ads, Purchases and Tracking

The App has no ads and does not include any ad SDK. There are no in-app purchases, no behaviorally targeted ads and no tracking.

5. External Analytics Tools

The App does not include any third-party analytics tool, including crash analytics or usage analytics.

If an external tool is introduced in the future for quality improvement, this policy will be updated before it is introduced. Even then, in accordance with the requirements of the kids category, it will be limited to a scope that does not collect or transmit advertising identifiers, information that could identify a child, location information or device information.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is designed for children and does not collect personal information from children online. We take into account the children’s privacy protection laws of each country and region, including Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information, COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in the EU.

7. Changing and Deleting Data

The ninja name can be individually changed or deleted from “なまえを かえる” (Change name) on the title screen. Progress can be reset from “最初から やりなおす” (Start over).

If you delete the App from the device, the App’s data stored on the device is deleted as well.

8. For Parents and Guardians

From “おうちのひと せってい” (Settings for grown-ups) on the title screen, press and hold the button for 3 seconds to open the parental settings. You can change the daily limit on the number of scrolls and the hours during which the game can be played.

The App can only manage use within the App itself. To manage screen time for the device as a whole, please use OS features such as Screen Time and Family Sharing.

9. Revisions to This Policy

If the App’s features, the handling of information, applicable laws or store requirements change, this policy will be updated and the change announced on this page. The date of the update is shown at the top of the page.
